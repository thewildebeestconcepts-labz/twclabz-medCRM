Install TWC MedCRM on Android

1. Tap the 3 dots (⋮) in Chrome at the top-right corner.
2. Select "Install App" or "Add to Home screen".
3. Tap "Install" in the popup to add the full-screen hospital CRM app.

Install on iPhone & iPad

1. Tap Safari's Share button at the bottom toolbar.
2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" .
3. Tap Add at top right to launch full-screen like a native app.
